# Design context: Sushiro

## Users
People queueing at Sushiro branches in Hong Kong, usually using a phone. The ticket collection flow records when they took a ticket and when it was called, while letting them check the current called numbers.

## Brand personality
Direct, compact, calm. Follow the existing Sushiro viewer's minimal visual system documented in CONTEXT.md.

## Aesthetic direction
Use the established light, high-contrast black-and-white interface, fine borders, flat surfaces, and existing bilingual system typography. Keep controls comfortably sized for touch and maintain Cantonese Traditional Chinese as the default, with English available.

## Design principles
- Make ticket entry short and easy to correct.
- Keep source sightings distinct from user-reported call times.
- Use visible labels and native form controls.
- Preserve progress after failed requests and when revisiting on the same browser.
- Make the form useful without an account or location access.
