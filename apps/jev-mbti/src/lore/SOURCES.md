# Lore library sources

The lore library paraphrases how Korean social media stereotypes MBTI types and letters. It records community perception and memes, not validated psychology. No post is copied verbatim; every line is rewritten in this project's own words.

## Method

Research took place on October 9, 2026, through Korean-language web searches. The sources below were read directly. The search service rate-limited further queries, so the remaining lines follow widely repeated community consensus: nicknames such as ENFP 댕댕이, the "너 T야?" and "나 우울해서 빵 샀어" T/F tests, 파워J/극P travel memes, 회식 and 2차 habits, and zombie-survival rankings.

16Personalities' official Korean type names are deliberately not used.

## Thinner areas

The research covered texting, T/F, J/P, and dating most directly. Lines about crisis and zombie survival, 회식 and 2차, money, and food rely on broadly repeated community stereotypes rather than a single cited post, and are the best candidates for review.
