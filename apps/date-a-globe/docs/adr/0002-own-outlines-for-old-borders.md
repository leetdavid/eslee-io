# Natural Earth plus our own outlines for old borders

Plates draw today's countries from Natural Earth, which is public domain, and draw any border that no longer exists (the Republic of Texas, the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, the inner-German border) from an outline we make per event, usually by cutting a modern shape along the old line. Ready-made historical border datasets exist and would save that work, but the main one is GPL-licensed and CShapes is non-commercial only, and neither licence fits shipping the shapes inside the app.

## Consequences

- Each event with an old border needs an outline before its plate is right; until then its plate shows the location without that border. Reviewing a submission includes drawing its outline.
