# Design context: Sushiro

## Users
People queueing at Sushiro branches in Hong Kong, usually using a phone. They want to know how long each branch's wait is now and how it has been trending. The ticket collection flow records when they took a ticket and when it was called.

## Brand personality
Direct, compact, calm. No Sushiro branding that could imply affiliation.

## Aesthetic direction
Version 2 uses Fluid Functionalism in its neutral form: grey layered surfaces, hairline shadows, rounded corners and spring motion. Queue bands (green through red) are the only colour. Light and dark both ship, switched by the theme toggle. Cantonese in Traditional Chinese is the default language, with English available.

The designs live on Doop canvas `_kQrdcpH9P`, page `sushiro v2`. The canvas style guide `sushiro-v2` holds the tokens and rules, and `src/app/globals.css` implements them. Terms follow `CONTEXT.md`.

## Design principles
- Lead with the official wait in minutes. Show waiting groups beside it, never in its place.
- Colour means queue. Everything else is neutral, and a band colour always sits next to a number or label.
- The branch grid keeps its six-column geographic order and its territory bands at every width.
- Use the installed Fluid Functionalism registry components (`src/components/ui`) for controls. Write plain classes on the shared tokens for app-specific pieces such as tiles, rows and charts.
- Make ticket entry short and easy to correct. Keep source sightings distinct from user-reported call times.
- Use visible labels and native form controls. Preserve progress after failed requests and on return visits.
- The app must be useful without an account or location access.

## Build status
Home, the store detail sheet, the app shell and the theme toggle are on version 2. Map and My tickets still use the version 1 styles carried at the end of `globals.css`. Stats is not built.
