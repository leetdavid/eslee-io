hong-kong.png was rasterized from hong-kong.svg at high resolution and composited into a single white land mask. District seams were closed; sea remains transparent.

The interactive map now renders hong-kong.svg directly at the zoomed display size
to retain vector detail. The PNG is a legacy raster export.

Geographic calibration for marker placement:
https://mapsvg.com/maps/geo-calibrated/hong-kong.svg

The original SVG declares mapsvg:geoViewBox="113.834541 22.562136 114.441018 22.153152"
and dimensions 612.52246 by 444.19733. Its paths use the same coordinate system as
the local SVG, whose viewBox was rounded up to 613 by 445.

Markers use MapSVG's Mercator conversion: longitude determines horizontal position
and the uniform projection scale from the original width. Mercator latitude is
anchored at the northern boundary. Coordinates are then expressed as percentages
of the local 613 by 445 viewBox. The bottom latitude is not used to stretch the
vertical scale. This preserves the source projection and the local image padding.

Store latitude and longitude come directly from Sushiro's store-list API. Marker
centers are not displaced to avoid overlaps. The underlying coastline is simplified
and its white mask has been dilated, so it is not a street-level basemap.
